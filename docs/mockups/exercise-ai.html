Single-leg Romanian deadlift

Hamstrings, glutes, balance · dumbbells

Hamstrings and glutes through a hip hinge, plus one-leg balance for cuts on court. Needs only dumbbells.

Suggested 3 × 8 per leg · start 2 × 10 kg
  1. Stand on one leg, knee soft, a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Hinge at the hip and reach the free leg straight back.
  3. Keep your hips square and your back flat.
  4. Drive through the standing heel to stand tall.

Common mistake: letting the back leg's hip open up and rotate.
