# Winter Training & Diet Plan

This is the plan Formkoll runs. Written 6 Nov 2026, updated 7 Nov 2026 (no breakfast). The structured version of the same values is in `seed/plan.json`.

## Goal and targets

Lose about 0.5 kg a week, from 88 kg to roughly 78 kg by mid-April 2027, while keeping the strength needed for bouldering and the speed needed for frisbee. That pace protects muscle and still allows training five days a week.

| Target | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pace | 0.4–0.7 kg per week (weekly average) |
| Calories, training days (Mon–Thu, Sat) | ~2,400 kcal |
| Calories, rest days (Fri, Sun) | ~2,000 kcal |
| Protein, every day | 160 g |
| Sessions per week | 1–2 frisbee, 2 bouldering, 2 gym |
| Steps | 8,000–10,000 a day (the commute covers a lot of it) |

The calories assume an active man of about 88 kg; height and age weren't given, so they are a starting point and the scale corrects them (see Tracking). Ending at 78–80 kg with more muscle may beat chasing 75. Decide in March.

## Tuesday-frisbee weeks

When playing on Tuesday as well, drop Gym A and keep Wednesday easy. Two late nights on top of four hard days in a row is how injuries and stalls happen.

| Day | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Bouldering 1 as usual |
| Tue | Frisbee 21:00–22:30 replaces Gym A. Stay in town: a real dinner near work around 18:00–18:30 and a small snack around 20:00 |
| Wed | Rest, or 60 min of easy technique climbing if fresh. No hard problems |
| Thu | Frisbee as usual |
| Fri | Rest |
| Sat | One full-body gym session: the first four exercises of Gym A, then the jumps and bike sprints from Gym B |

For the first six weeks, keep double-frisbee weeks to about two a month.

## Gym sessions

Two 50–60 minute sessions that build the legs and core frisbee needs and the pushing muscles climbing leaves out. No extra pulling work, because bouldering covers it.

- **Effort:** end every set with about 2 reps still in the tank.
- **Progression:** when every set reaches the top of its rep range, add 2.5 kg next time (or 1–2 reps on bodyweight moves).
- **Deload:** every 4th week, 2 sets of each exercise at the same weights.
- **Warm-up (8 min, both days):** 4 min on the bike or rower; leg swings, a deep lunge with rotation, hip circles; 15 band pull-aparts; 2 light ramp-up sets of the first lift.

**Gym A: strength (Tuesday)**

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trap-bar deadlift or back squat | 4 × 5 | Strength for sprinting and jumping |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 8 per leg | Single-leg strength for cuts |
| Dumbbell bench press | 3 × 8 | Pushing, to balance climbing shoulders |
| Nordic hamstring curl (or lying leg curl) | 3 × 4–6, slow on the way down | Protects against hamstring strains |
| Half-kneeling one-arm overhead press | 3 × 8 per arm | Shoulder health |
| Copenhagen plank | 3 × 20–30 s per side | Protects against groin strains |
| Calf raise, straight knee and bent knee | 2 × 12 each | Achilles and ankles for court sprints |

**Gym B: power and conditioning (Saturday)**

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Box jump or broad jump | 4 × 3, full rest | Explosiveness. Stop before the jumps get slower |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 | Hamstrings and hips |
| Lateral lunge | 3 × 6 per side | Side-to-side cutting |
| Push-ups (add weight when easy) or dips | 3 × 8–12 | Pushing |
| Pallof press | 3 × 10 per side | Core control for throwing and marking |
| Band external rotation, then reverse wrist curl | 2 × 15 each | Rotator cuff and elbows for climbing |
| Bike or rower sprints | 8 × 20 s hard, 70 s easy | Fitness for repeated sprints, like back-to-back points |

## Bouldering sessions

Climb twice a week, at least 48 hours apart: Monday hard, Wednesday moderate. Fingers recover more slowly in a calorie deficit, so no hangboard this winter.

**Warm-up for both sessions (15–20 min):** an easy traverse or 6–8 problems well below max, working up through the grades. Add arm circles and band pull-aparts. Open-hand grips before any small crimps.

**Bouldering 1: hard problems (Monday, ~100 min)**

1. Warm up as above.
2. 45–60 min on 3–5 problems at or near the hardest grade. 3–5 good attempts each, 3–4 min rest between attempts.
3. Finish with 10 min: 2 × 10 push-ups, 2 × 8 hanging knee raises, then stretch forearms and chest.

**Bouldering 2: volume and technique (Wednesday, ~90 min)**

1. Warm up as above.
2. Alternate between two kinds of week:
    - Technique weeks: repeat problems 2–3 grades below max with one focus each time, such as silent feet, flagging, or hips close to the wall.
    - Volume weeks: 4×4s, which means 4 problems back to back, 4 rounds, with 3–4 min rest between rounds.
3. Stop while still feeling sharp, because Thursday needs fresh legs.

Sharp pain at the base of a finger, or soreness there the next morning: at least 3 days off climbing and no crimps for a week after. See a physio if it doesn't settle. Eat the 15:30 snack before climbing.

## Nutrition targets

About 2,400 kcal on training days and 2,000 kcal on rest days, with 160 g of protein every day. That averages about 2,280 kcal a day, roughly 500 below maintenance.

| Per day | Training days | Rest days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | ~2,400 kcal | ~2,000 kcal |
| Protein | 160 g | 160 g |
| Carbs | ~280 g | ~180 g |
| Fat | ~70 g | ~70 g |
| Fibre | 30 g or more | 30 g or more |
| Water | 2.5–3 L, plus ~0.5 L per hour of training | ~2.5 L |

- **Day type follows what was actually done.** A Tuesday-frisbee week has 4 training days and 3 rest days.
- **Protein:** spread over lunch, the afternoon snack, dinner and an evening serving, 30–55 g each.
- **Plate rule:** 1–2 palms of protein, 1–2 fists of carbs (2 on training days, 1 on rest days), half the plate vegetables, 1 thumb of fat.
- **Alcohol:** a 33 cl beer is about 150 kcal and worsens sleep. Weekends only, 2–3 drinks at most.

## Winter notes

- **Vitamin D:** Livsmedelsverket's recommended intake for adults is 10 µg a day, 20 µg with little sun. Fatty fish twice a week plus fortified milk, or a 10–20 µg tablet October to March. Stay under 100 µg a day (EFSA upper limit).
- **Daylight:** walk 20–30 min at lunch on office days and on Thursday, plus the long Sunday walk.
- **Sleep:** 7.5–9 hours. Protect Friday and Sunday nights.
- **Cold muscles:** add 5 min to every warm-up after a cold trip.
- **Colds:** above-the-neck symptoms only, an easy session or walk is fine. Fever, body aches or a chest cough: no training until 24 hours fever-free.
- **Christmas break (21 Dec–4 Jan):** two weeks at maintenance, about 2,750 kcal, protein 160 g, keep training. A 1–2 kg jump afterwards is mostly water and salt.

## Tracking and adjusting

Judge progress by the weekly average weight, and only change calories after two weeks of evidence. At 0.5 kg a week: about 85 kg by 21 Dec, hold over Christmas, about 81 kg by early March, 77–78 kg by mid-April 2027.

- Weigh 3–4 mornings a week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun), after the toilet and before eating.
- Waist at the navel every 2 weeks.
- One performance marker per sport: hardest problem sent, Gym A main-lift weight, how sprints feel late in frisbee.
- Expect 1–2 kg off in week 1 (water and stored carbs); it doesn't count toward the pace.

| If this holds for 2 weeks in a row | Then |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly average drops by less than 0.3 kg | Cut 150 kcal from rest-day carbs, or add 2,000 steps a day |
| Drops by more than 1 kg (after week 2) | Add 150–200 kcal on training days |
| Climbing grades or lifts going down, poor sleep, constantly hungry | Add 200 kcal on training days and take a deload week |
| Waist shrinking but scale flat | Change nothing (adding muscle) |
